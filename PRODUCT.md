# Atlas PO ERP

## Users

Purchasing staff, approvers, warehouse staff, branch receivers and accounting at businesses that buy from suppliers for several branches. This could be a retail chain, a distributor, a hardware or grocery group, or any multi-branch business in the Philippines. They work at office PCs during the day, entering order forms with many rows from supplier quotes, and they expect fast keyboard entry.

## Product purpose

Take a purchase from order to payment without spreadsheets: order forms, approval, branch POs, warehouse receiving, blind branch counts, debit memos and claim stubs, and supplier checks. Accuracy matters most. The business must never overpay a supplier, and every record stays on file.

## Register

Product. Staff work in these screens all day, so scanability and keyboard speed come first.

## Tone

Plain, exact and calm. Labels use everyday purchasing words (OF, PO, DR, supplier, department, branch).

## Visual reference

Light neutral surfaces, one teal accent and Segoe UI Variable. Light mode is the default; dark mode uses the same roles and is chosen per Windows user.

## Anti-references

Generic admin templates with hero metric cards, and decoration that slows data entry.
