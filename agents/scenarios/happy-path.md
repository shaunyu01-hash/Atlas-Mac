# Scenario: happy path

One order, everything goes right. Fill in the details.

1. Supplier: sends a quote (no VAT) with payment terms.
2. Purchaser: enters the Order Form, submits it.
3. Management: approves it (needed when the whole order is above ₱100,000).
4. Purchaser: prints and issues all the branch POs of the OF together from the Print tab.
5. Supplier: delivers everything to Central Warehouse and writes the DRs.
6. Warehouse receiver: records the boxes per branch PO (and stickered pieces if the supplier has a sticker deduction), then ships to the branches. That day starts the payment terms.
7. Branch receivers (each branch, counters 1 and 2): count blind until both counts agree.
8. Accounting: compares the DRs, works out the check and the due Saturday, records the check, then marks it claimed when the supplier takes it.
9. Auditor: checks it all adds up.
